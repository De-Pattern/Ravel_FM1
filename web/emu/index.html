Audio in

The FM-1 has no audio input in Ravel (its USB sends Ravel's output to a computer). Here a file stands in, so you can ring a strike body with the EXT exciter.

MIDI in

Notes, clock and start/stop from a keyboard or a DAW, as the FM-1 takes them over USB or its TRS jack (GLO › MIDI IN sets the channel and the clock).

Projects & sounds

Saved projects (SAVE › PROJECT) and sound banks (SAVE › SOUNDS) move as a file, or straight to an FM-1 running Ravel over its USB cable (Chrome or Edge: allow MIDI when asked).

WEFT patcher

A WEFT track is a voice you patch from eight blocks: oscillators, slopes, filters, logic, arithmetic, a short delay, bands. Make a track WEFT here or on the FM-1 (EDIT › SOUND › KIND), then patch it below: drag from a block's output to another's input.

Drag from a block's output (the dot on its right) to an input (A or B, on the left); a cord back to an earlier block is feedback, a sample late (green). Click a cord to take it out. The built-in sources (PITCH, GATE, NOISE, the LFOs…) are in the input menus. Edits play at once, here and on the FM-1's WEFT pages. To put a patch on your FM-1: save it as a sound or in the project, then Send to FM-1; Export patch keeps it as a file to share.
