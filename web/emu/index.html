Eight tracks

T1–T4 synthesize · T5–T6 strike · T7–T8 drum — any track can be any kind, PERC drum machines included, and every one can act on every other. Click a track to work on it.

WEFT patcher

A WEFT track is a voice you patch from eight blocks: oscillators, slopes, filters, logic, arithmetic, a short delay, bands. Make a track WEFT here or on the FM-1 (EDIT › SOUND › KIND), then patch it below: drag from a block's output to another's input.

Drag from a block's output (the dot on its right) to an input (A or B, on the left); a cord back to an earlier block is feedback, a sample late (dashed). Click a cord to take it out. The built-in sources (PITCH, GATE, NOISE, the LFOs…) are in the input menus. Edits play at once, here and on the FM-1's WEFT pages. To put a patch on your FM-1: save it as a sound or in the project, then Send to FM-1; Export patch keeps it as a file to share.
